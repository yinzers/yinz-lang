# Capability Ledger: v0.1 Compiler

Every capability this initiative delivered maps to an owning milestone. All milestones shipped (v0.1.0 tag cut); the Notes column records what each done milestone delivered (the done-child ownership record that stands in for retroactive per-phase Scope Boundary blocks). This initiative was promoted to roadmap form during the ledger-format migration (the original `v0-1-compiler.md` carried no `type: roadmap`; its children declared no parent).
